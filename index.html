How we work

02

03

04

Fit

Who this is for, and who it is not for.

The best projects start where there is already meaningful work, useful data, and a clear reason to improve output or add capability.

Good fit

Firms that want controlled, useful AI systems tied to real work.

  • Professional firms with recurring workflows and decision bottlenecks
  • Operators who want measurable output, not AI theater
  • Teams that need more capacity without immediately adding headcount
  • Leaders who want human-in-the-loop controls where risk matters

Not a fit

Situations where the goal is novelty, not operational value.

  • Firms looking for a generic chatbot dropped onto the website
  • Teams that want full autopilot with no oversight
  • Projects with no real owner, workflow, or business use case
  • One-off experiments that are not meant to run in production

Start with one workflow that matters.

If there is a process in your firm that is repetitive, slow, inconsistent, or simply not getting done, that is the right place to start.

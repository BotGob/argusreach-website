The problem

The work is not one task. It is the coordination around the task.

Most teams do not need another AI app. They need the manual work around spreadsheets, PDFs, CRMs, shared folders, reports, approvals, and status updates to become a system.

Where useful AI usually starts

Every week or month, someone pulls information from several places, cleans it, checks it, rebuilds a report, explains what changed, and sends the same updates again. That is the workflow we look for first.

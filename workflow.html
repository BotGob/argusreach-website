How we work

Custom work, built around how you already run.

We talk through the work you do now and the work you wish you could get to, then help you see where AI can handle it for a fraction of what a new hire or an agency would cost.

Nothing to set up You don't install or manage anything. We do.
You approve the work Results come to you to approve, change, or send back.
Days or weeks Working in days or weeks, not a long IT project.
Process

From first call to running on its own.

Here is what happens after you book.

01

Discovery call

We meet by phone, video or in person and mostly listen. Walk us through your week, or come with something specific in mind. We take notes either way.

  • A workflow or job you want off your plate
  • A role you're trying to hire for or can't fill
  • Something you've always wanted to do but haven't known how
02

Opportunities follow-up

We come back and walk you through where a digital employee can take work off your plate, what each one would take, and what it frees up.

  • Built from what you told us on the first call
  • Ranked by how much of your week each gives back
  • Honest calls on what isn't worth automating
03

You choose

Based on your needs, timeline and budget, you pick what to build. One opportunity or all of them, now or in stages.

  • You set the order and the pace
  • Add more whenever you're ready
04

Map it

You show us, or tell us, what a great finished result looks like. Then we map the whole job from start to finish.

  • Where the data comes from and the tools you use today
  • Every step and decision between the raw data and the finished result
  • Where it helps, a smarter way to run the work, keeping your data sources and the output you want
05

Build, test, review

We connect to where your data lives, build it, test it on real work and review the results with you before it goes live.

  • Custom software, scoped to your business
  • Tested on real work, not samples
  • Adjusted until it works the way you want
06

Live and running

It goes live and does the work on schedule, whether that's daily, weekly, monthly or quarterly. We keep it running and improve it when it's needed.

  • Monitored, so problems get caught early
  • Results delivered wherever works best for you
  • More time back for your customers and the decisions that matter

  • Finished drafts of the work that used to eat your week

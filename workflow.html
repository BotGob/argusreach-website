How we work

One real job. Built into a digital employee.

We start with one job inside your business, learn how it runs today, and build the digital employee around it.

Nothing to set up You don't install or manage anything. Argus does.
You approve the work Results come to you to approve, change, or send back.
Days or weeks Useful capacity in days or weeks, not a long IT project.
Process

Talk. Map. Build. Keep it running.

Here is what happens after you book.

01

Talk

It starts with a 30-minute conversation about your bottlenecks. Then we come back with a few ideas.

  • Where the hours go each week
  • What keeps slipping or never gets done
  • Which job pays off first
02

Map

Before we build anything, we walk through the job with you, step by step.

  • Where the information comes from
  • What the finished result looks like
  • Where you review and approve
03

Build

We build the digital employee around how your team already works.

  • Connected to the email, files, and software the job already uses
  • Checks at each step
  • Tested on real examples before it goes live
04

Keep it running

After launch, we stay on it.

  • We check the work and catch problems early
  • We adjust it when the job or your rules change
  • We take on the next job once the first one runs well
In and out

What goes in. What comes back to you.

A digital employee works from the information you already have and hands back finished work.

What it works from

The places your information already lives.

  • Spreadsheets and files
  • Your CRM and other software
  • Email inboxes
  • Websites and outside sources worth watching

What lands on your desk

Finished work, ready for you to review.

  • Reports and summaries, like the Monday sales report
  • Drafted follow-ups and to-dos
  • A heads-up when something needs your attention
  • The work you kept putting off

What We Do

What ArgusReach actually builds

We build focused AI systems around real business processes. That can mean an agent, dashboard, recurring report, internal portal, document extraction flow, CRM/reporting tool, data cleanup tool, SharePoint/Excel workflow, or automation that removes manual drag from the work.
It is both, with a build bias. We identify the workflow, then build the system. The goal is not a strategy deck. The goal is a useful operating layer that improves reporting, reduces friction, or makes a recurring process easier to run.
Small and mid-sized businesses with workflows that are still too manual. The fit is strongest when the business has recurring reports, research, data pulls, admin processes, client communication, or operational work that people keep rebuilding by hand.

Timing & Pricing

How the build is scoped

The timeline depends on complexity, data quality, access, and review cycles. We scope one focused workflow first, then estimate the build after we understand the systems, files, and rules involved.
Pricing depends on the workflow, the systems involved, the amount of custom build work, and whether hosting, refreshes, or maintenance are needed. Some work is project-based. Some clients add ongoing support once the first useful system is live.

Security & Reliability

How the system stays trustworthy

We do not position AI as replacing staff, promise exact time savings, sell fully autonomous agents, or pretend every process should run without review. The work is AI-assisted while humans stay involved where judgment matters.
We define rules, review points, logs, source expectations, and a maintenance path before a workflow is trusted with recurring work. The system should be inspectable, not mysterious.
We keep data access as narrow as possible. Live client data should not be placed in public repos or casual shared documents. For recurring systems, we define where files live, how they are ingested, what gets validated, and who owns updates.

What We Do
We build useful AI systems around real business workflows. That can mean an AI agent, interactive dashboard, recurring management report, internal portal, document extraction flow, CRM/reporting system, or automation layer that reduces manual work.

Workflow
A good first project is painful, repeatable, and clear enough to inspect. Examples include monthly management reporting, spreadsheet-to-dashboard workflows, recurring data collection, document extraction, CRM/pipeline reporting, finance/admin reviews, or operational status reporting.

Timing & Pricing
The timeline depends on complexity, data quality, access, and review cycles. We usually start by scoping one focused workflow first, then estimate the build after we understand the systems, files, and business rules involved.
Pricing depends on the scope of the workflow, the systems involved, the amount of custom build work, and whether ongoing hosting, refreshes, or maintenance are needed. We discuss pricing after the workflow is understood.
Yes. Some systems are one-time builds. Others need recurring data refreshes, hosting, validation, reporting changes, or ongoing support. We define the maintenance path before launch so the system is not fragile.
Security
We keep data access as narrow as possible. Live client data should not be placed in public repos or casual shared documents. For recurring systems, we define where files live, how they are ingested, what gets validated, and who is responsible for updates.

FAQ

Straight answers for business owners.

What it is, what it costs, how long it takes, and who does what.

Best way to start

Bring one job that eats hours every week, keeps slipping, or never gets done. That is usually the right first digital employee.

  • A weekly report someone has to pull together
  • Follow-ups with clients or leads that slip
  • Paperwork and admin that piles up
  • A check that should happen every day but doesn't

What is a digital employee?

Something we build that does a repetitive computer job for your business, like the Monday sales report or lead follow-ups. The finished work comes to you to approve.

Do I have to set it up or run it?

No. Argus maps the job, builds it, and keeps it running. Your part is reviewing the work and making the calls.

What does it cost?

It depends on the job. Work is part-time or project-based, usually a fraction of the cost of a full-time hire, and you see the price before any work starts.

How long does it take?

Days or weeks, depending on the job. We get one job working before we add the next.

What do you need from us?

A walkthrough of how the job runs today, access to the email, files, or software it touches, and someone to review the results. You keep the systems you already use.

What happens to our data?

Your data stays in your systems. We use business AI plans that do not train on your data. Every access is yours to revoke.

Who is this a good fit for?

Small and mid-size businesses with repetitive computer work that eats the week, like law and accounting offices, property managers, practices, and contractors. It is not a fit if you want a chatbot or a setup nobody reviews.
